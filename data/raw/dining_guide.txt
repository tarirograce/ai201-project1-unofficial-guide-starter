Northgate University — Unofficial Dining Guide

Where to eat on and around campus, ranked by hungry students who have tried it all.

Dining halls:
Riverside Dining Hall is the largest and has the most variety. It is the best dining hall for vegetarian and vegan options — there is a dedicated plant-based station that rotates daily, plus a salad bar that is actually fresh. The tofu stir fry on Wednesdays is a campus favorite. Riverside also labels allergens clearly, which is a relief if you have dietary restrictions.

Hilltop Dining Hall is smaller and closer to the science buildings. It is convenient if you have back-to-back labs, but the menu is repetitive. Stick to the grill station, which is reliable. Avoid the soup, which students jokingly call "mystery broth."

Coffee and snacks:
The Library Cafe has the best coffee on campus and stays open until midnight during finals. The Quad Kiosk is cheaper but the espresso machine is hit or miss.

Off campus:
Tony's Pizza, two blocks from the north gate, is the go-to late night spot. They take meal plan dollars after 9pm. Green Bowl does build-your-own grain bowls and is the healthiest option nearby. For a treat, the Sunday brunch at the Corner Diner is worth the wait, but get there before 10am or the line wraps around the block.

Money saving tips: the meal plan dollars expire at the end of each semester, so spend them down in the last two weeks. Many off-campus spots offer a student discount if you show your ID — always ask. The dining halls do free refills on coffee, so bring a travel mug.
