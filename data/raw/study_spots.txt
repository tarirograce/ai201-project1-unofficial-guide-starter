Northgate University — Best Study Spots (Unofficial)

A student-curated list of where to actually get work done.

Quiet study spots:
The third and fourth floors of the Main Library are designated silent floors. This is the quietest place on campus, no talking allowed, individual carrels with outlets. If you need deep focus for an exam, go here. Arrive early during finals week because seats disappear by 9am.

The Law Library is technically for law students but is open to everyone and is almost always empty in the mornings. It is gorgeous, very quiet, and has comfortable chairs.

Group study and collaboration:
The Learning Commons on the first floor of the Main Library has whiteboards, movable furniture, and reservable group rooms. Book a room online up to a week in advance. This is the spot for group projects.

The Student Union has couches and a louder, more social vibe. Good for casual reading or working while waiting between classes, not for serious focus.

Hidden gems:
The greenhouse study nook in the Biology building is a secret favorite — lots of natural light, plants everywhere, and hardly anyone knows about it. The Music building practice lounge is quiet during the day if you do not mind occasional piano in the background.

Tips: outlets are scarce in older buildings, so bring a portable charger. The library gets cold, bring a layer. Coffee is allowed everywhere except the special collections room. During finals, the library extends hours to 24 hours a day for the last two weeks.
