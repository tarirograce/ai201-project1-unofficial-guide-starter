Northgate University — Unofficial Professor Reviews

Honest, student-sourced notes on professors. Opinions, not gospel.

Professor Alvarez (Intro to Computer Science): One of the best-liked professors on campus. Her lectures are clear, she posts all slides and recordings, and the weekly labs build directly on the homework. Class advice: start the projects early — they are not hard but they take longer than you expect. Office hours are genuinely helpful and rarely crowded on Fridays. Exams are fair and drawn straight from practice problems.

Professor Chen (Organic Chemistry): Brilliant but fast. You must read before lecture or you will be lost. He curves generously at the end of the semester, so do not panic over a low midterm. Form a study group early; the problem sets are too much to tackle alone.

Professor Okafor (Microeconomics): Engaging lecturer who uses a lot of real-world examples. Attendance is not mandatory but the exams include material only covered in class. The textbook is optional — his lecture notes are enough.

Professor Reyes (Introduction to Philosophy): Heavy reading load, around eighty pages a week. Grades are based almost entirely on three essays, no exams. If you are a strong writer you will do well. He gives detailed feedback on the first essay, so use it.

General tips: check the unofficial course rating site before registering, but weight recent reviews more heavily because teaching styles change. Always go to the first lecture before deciding to drop — syllabi tell you a lot. Professors notice when you come to office hours, and it helps at grading time for borderline cases.
