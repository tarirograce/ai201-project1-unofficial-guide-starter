Northgate University — Unofficial Housing Reviews

This is a student-written rundown of the dorms at Northgate. Take it with a grain of salt, but most of it holds up year after year.

Best dorm for first-year students: Maple Hall. It is hands down the most recommended building for incoming freshmen. The rooms are larger than average, every floor has a study lounge, and the resident advisors actually organize events that help you meet people. It is a five minute walk to the main dining hall and ten minutes to the library. The only downside is that the laundry machines fill up fast on Sunday nights, so do your laundry on a weekday.

Cedar Hall is the party dorm. It is loud, it is social, and if you want to make friends quickly it is great. If you want to sleep before 1am on weekends, look elsewhere. The walls are thin and the heating is unpredictable in winter.

Birchwood Apartments are for upperclassmen. You get a private kitchen and your own bathroom, which is a huge upgrade after a year in a double. They are a bit far from the academic quad, about a fifteen minute walk, but the quiet and privacy are worth it. Apply early because spots go fast in the spring lottery.

Pine Tower has the best views and the worst elevators. The elevators break down constantly, so if you get assigned to the twelfth floor, prepare for some cardio. The rooms themselves are clean and modern.

General advice: bring a mattress topper no matter which dorm you end up in, the standard mattresses are thin. A small fan is essential because most dorms do not have air conditioning and September is hot. Command strips, not nails, for decorating. And befriend your RA early — they can help with roommate conflicts and maintenance requests.
