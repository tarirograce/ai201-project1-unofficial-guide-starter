Northgate University — Course Registration Survival Guide (Unofficial)

Everything students wish they knew about registering for classes.

Registration timing: registration opens by class standing, with seniors going first and first-years last. Your exact time slot appears in the student portal about a week before. Log in the moment your slot opens — popular classes fill within minutes.

Before registration: meet with your academic advisor early. You usually need an advising hold lifted before you can register, and advisors get booked solid right before registration week. Build your ideal schedule plus two backup schedules, because something will inevitably be full.

Waitlists: get on the waitlist even if a class looks hopeless. Schedules shuffle a lot in the first two weeks as people add and drop. Email the professor expressing interest — many will sign an override for a motivated student, especially if you show up to the first class.

Add/drop period: you have two weeks at the start of each semester to add or drop without penalty. Use this time to shop around. Attend more classes than you plan to keep, then drop the ones that are not a fit. After the add/drop deadline, withdrawing leaves a W on your transcript.

Requirements: keep your own checklist of degree requirements rather than trusting memory. The degree audit tool in the portal shows what you still need. Knock out general education requirements early so your later years are flexible.

Tips: balance hard and easy courses each semester rather than stacking all your difficult classes together. Avoid back-to-back exams by checking the final exam schedule before you register.
