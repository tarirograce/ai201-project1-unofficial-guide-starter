Northgate University — Clubs and Organizations (Unofficial)

There are over two hundred student organizations at Northgate. Here is how to navigate them.

How to join: the Activities Fair happens the second week of the fall semester on the main quad. Every active club sets up a table. Bring a phone to sign up for mailing lists, but do not feel obligated to commit on the spot. You can join clubs any time during the year, not just at the fair.

Popular clubs students recommend:
The Outdoors Club organizes weekend hiking and camping trips, gear included, and is one of the easiest ways to make friends fast. The Coding Club runs weekly project nights and a beginner-friendly hackathon each spring. The Debate Society is competitive and travels to tournaments. The International Students Association hosts cultural nights with free food that are open to everyone.

Getting involved deeper: student government meets every Tuesday evening and is always looking for first-year representatives. Club leadership positions usually open up in the spring; express interest early to the current officers.

Funding: registered clubs can apply for funding from the student activities board each semester. The application opens in the first month and the deadline sneaks up, so plan ahead if your club needs money for events.

Starting your own club: you need a faculty advisor, a constitution, and ten interested students. The student activities office walks you through it. It takes about a month to get officially recognized.

Tip: do not overcommit. Two or three clubs you care about beats ten you never attend.
