Northgate University — Campus Safety (Unofficial Guide)

Staying safe on and around campus.

Campus Safety operates 24/7 and their non-emergency number is worth saving in your phone. For emergencies, the blue light emergency phones are posted throughout campus — press the button and an officer is dispatched immediately to that location even if you cannot speak.

Walking at night: use the free SafeWalk program. Call campus safety and an officer or trained student will walk you anywhere on campus after dark. There is also the on-demand evening shuttle that picks you up and drops you off at any campus location.

The Northgate Safety app is the single best tool. It has a panic button, live shuttle tracking, a friend-walk feature that lets a friend follow your location until you arrive, and instant alerts about incidents on campus. Download it during orientation.

Protecting your stuff: theft of unattended laptops in the library is the most common crime on campus. Never leave belongings unattended, even for a minute. Register your bike and laptop with campus safety so they can be returned if recovered. Lock your dorm room even when you step out briefly.

Emergency alerts: make sure your phone number is registered for the emergency alert system through the student portal. This is how the university notifies you about weather closures, lockdowns, or other urgent situations. Test alerts go out once a semester.

Know where you are: every building and emergency phone has a location code. If you call for help, knowing that code speeds up the response.
