Northgate University — Health and Wellness (Unofficial)

Taking care of yourself at Northgate.

The Student Health Center is on the ground floor of Maple Hall and is free for enrolled students for most visits. Book appointments through the health portal; walk-ins are accepted for urgent issues but expect a wait. They handle illnesses, vaccinations, and basic prescriptions. The on-campus pharmacy fills prescriptions cheaply.

Mental health: counseling services are free and confidential. The first appointment can take a week or two to schedule during busy periods, so reach out before things become a crisis. They offer individual sessions, group therapy, and drop-in workshops on stress and sleep. There is also a 24/7 crisis line staffed by trained counselors — the number is on the back of your student ID.

Fitness: the Recreation Center is free with your student ID. It has a gym, a pool, climbing wall, and free group fitness classes like yoga and spin. Classes fill up, so reserve a spot in the app. The intramural sports leagues are casual and a fun way to stay active and meet people.

Sleep and stress: the most common student health mistake is skipping sleep during exams. The library has nap pods you can reserve. The wellness office runs free stress-management workshops before finals.

Insurance: if you are on the university health plan, most campus services are fully covered. If you are on a parent's plan, check what is in-network before going off campus.
