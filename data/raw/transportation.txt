Northgate University — Getting Around (Unofficial Transportation Guide)

How to get around campus, downtown, and beyond without losing your mind or your money.

Getting downtown without a car:
The campus shuttle is free with your student ID and is the easiest way to get from campus to downtown. The Blue Line shuttle runs every twenty minutes from the Student Union to the downtown transit center from 7am to 11pm on weekdays, and every forty minutes on weekends. The trip takes about fifteen minutes. Download the NorthgateTransit app to see live arrival times.

The city bus Route 12 also connects campus to downtown and runs later than the shuttle, until about 1am. It costs a dollar fifty, but students ride free with a validated ID sticker that you pick up from the transit office.

Biking is popular and the dedicated bike path along the river gets you downtown in about ten minutes. The university has a bike share program, twenty dollars a semester for unlimited thirty minute rides.

Around campus:
Everything on the main campus is walkable in under fifteen minutes. There is also a free on-demand evening safety shuttle that will pick you up anywhere on campus after dark — call campus safety or use the app.

Getting to the airport:
The regional airport is forty minutes away. The cheapest option is the Route 12 bus to the transit center, then the airport express. A rideshare runs about thirty-five dollars. During breaks, the university organizes a discounted airport shuttle — sign up a week in advance through the student portal.

Parking: do not bring a car your first year unless you must. Parking permits are expensive and freshman permits are restricted to a distant lot.
